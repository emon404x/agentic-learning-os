# Teaching Styles & Bilingual Protocol

## 9 Supported Teaching Styles
1. `beginner`: Concrete everyday analogies first, zero undefined jargon, tiny examples.
2. `detailed`: Full internal state transitions, edge cases, and failure modes.
3. `socratic`: Guide the learner with sequence of discovery questions before summarizing.
4. `simple`: Short sentences, plain language, visual mental models.
5. `university`: Formal definitions, invariants, asymptotic complexity, derivations, and citations.
6. `professional`: Production trade-offs, observability, security, and maintainability.
7. `exam`: High-yield definitions, step-by-step worked math/analytical problems, and exam traps.
8. `practical_first`: Run or trace a concrete example first, then extract the rule.
9. `theory_first`: State the formal model/invariant first, then derive the implementation.

## Bilingual Teaching Rule (Bangla `bn` & English `en`)
When teaching in Bangla (`--lang bn`):
- Write the explanation, analogy, and walkthrough in clear, natural Bangla.
- Always preserve standard English technical terms (`Pointer`, `Subnetting`, `Deadlock`, `Normalization`, `Recursion`).
- Include both the Bangla meaning and the concise English technical definition.
