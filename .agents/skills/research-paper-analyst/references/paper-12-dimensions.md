# 12-Dimension Academic Paper Analysis Specification

Every research paper analyzed by `research-paper-analyst` must cover:
1. **Research Problem**: What concrete bottleneck or question the paper addresses.
2. **Background**: Prerequisite concepts needed to understand the paper.
3. **Research Gap**: Why existing approaches were insufficient.
4. **Related Work**: How prior literature is categorized and contrasted.
5. **Methodology**: Step-by-step explanation of the proposed model, algorithm, or theorem.
6. **Dataset**: Data sources, scale, preprocessing, and representativeness.
7. **Experimental Setup**: Baselines, evaluation metrics, hardware/hyperparameters.
8. **Results**: Quantitative findings (always framed as the authors' reported benchmark claims).
9. **Figures and Tables**: Walkthrough of key architecture diagrams and ablation tables.
10. **Limitations**: Assumptions, failure modes, and threats to validity.
11. **Contributions**: The core takeaways claimed by the authors.
12. **Future Research**: Open problems and concrete next experiments.
