# Multi-Criteria Assessment Rubric

Submitted answers are evaluated across six dimensions:
1. **Correctness**: Accuracy of the final result, output, or selected option.
2. **Conceptual Understanding**: Grasping the underlying invariant rather than surface memorization.
3. **Completeness**: Addressing all parts of the prompt, including boundary conditions.
4. **Reasoning**: Clear step-by-step derivation or architectural justification.
5. **Appropriate Terminology**: Accurate use of standard domain vocabulary.
6. **Practical Implementation**: Feasibility, complexity, and safety of the proposed code or design.

> **Formative Assessment Notice**: Scores generated by Stardi AI are diagnostic study aids to identify knowledge gaps and are never an official examination grade.
